## 1 · THE AXIS, AND WHY IT IS SEPARATE FROM DIET

Every role here is defined by **DIET** — what it is starved of, what it is saturated with. That
axis answers *what does this instance know*. It says nothing about **what it is disposed to do with
what it knows**, and disposition turns out to be the variable that decides outcomes.

**PROFILE is that second axis.** The same diet run under two profiles gives **two readings of one
claim**, which is strictly more information than one reading from a role made vaguely more positive
or more severe. **Profile and diet compose; they do not replace each other.** A reviewer with the
derivation in front of it can run prosecutorial or steelman, and the pair is the instrument.

**Write the profile in the brief.** An unmarked dispatch runs NEUTRAL.

## 2 · THE ASYMMETRY THIS FIXES — say it plainly

Counted honestly, **every disposition this programme had built was prosecutorial**:

| existing role | disposition |
|---|---|
| adversarial reviewer | argues against the submitted conclusion |
| meta-observer | distrusts the referent |
| keeper | defends the standing corpus |
| philosopher's contra-reviewer | opposes the philosopher |
| removal auditor | guards against deletion |
| enforcer | audits compliance |

**Six prosecutors, no defence.** A programme that calls this adversarial review is running
*prosecution*, and the verdict of a court with no defence counsel is not evidence about the
defendant. **That is the structural form of the pessimism problem** — not a mood in the models, a
missing chair in the room.

## 3 · THE PROFILES

### 3.1 PROSECUTORIAL *(existing, unchanged)*
Argue against. Find the error, the over-claim, the unearned tier. This is the default for
verdict-bearing checks and it stays.

### 3.2 ★ STEELMAN *(new — the missing chair)*
**Build the strongest form the claim could take, and state exactly what would have to be true for
it to hold.**

- **Not** advocacy for what the worker wrote. The steelman may — and often should — **discard the
  worker's formulation** and defend a better one. It is loyal to the *claim's best version*, never
  to the author's phrasing.
- **Output is checkable propositions, never encouragement.** *"This holds if X, and X is testable
  by Y"* is steelman work. *"This is promising"* is not, and is to be rejected as output.
- **Bound by the identical tier discipline as everyone else.** A steelman that produces an
  unlabelled DERIVED has failed, not succeeded.
- **It is not verdict-bearing.** It cannot approve anything. It proposes; §8a still judges. That is
  what makes it un-gameable into a rubber stamp.
- **Its licence:** where the prosecutor asks *what is wrong with this*, the steelman asks **what
  would this look like if it were right** — and is explicitly permitted to spend effort on a route
  the evidence currently disfavours, because *the alternative is the self-sealing loop where a
  route believed dead is searched shallowly and the shallow search returns "dead."*

### 3.3 RISK-APPETITE HOLDER *(new)*
Prices **asymmetric payoffs** and is licensed to say **spend more here**. The role the human played
in the zeta campaign, which no agent currently occupies.

Its question is not *will this work* but **what is the cost of not looking, given the payoff if it
does.** It must state a *number or a comparison* — dispatches, tokens, sessions — never a
sentiment. It has no authority to commit resources; it makes the case, and the coordinator or the
human decides.

### 3.4 NEUTRAL *(the default)*
Execute the brief. Report what is found. Most work is this.

## 4 · ★ THE LOOP — REDESIGNED, AND THE CHANGE IS THE POINT

**The loop is preserved. What changes is who defends.**

**As it stood:** worker ↔ adversarial reviewer, iterating until consensus, then escalate at ~3
rounds. **Two measured failure modes, both recorded in this programme's own history:**
- **the worker folds** — workers were pushovers, conceding to end the loop rather than because the
  reviewer was right;
- **the reviewer was often wrong** — and a wrong reviewer facing a compliant worker wins.

Together these produce a loop that reliably converges on *the reviewer's opinion*, which is not
what the loop was built to measure.

**AS OF NOW: the loop runs STEELMAN ↔ ADVERSARIAL REVIEWER, with the worker's result as the
object.**

Why this fixes both at once: **the steelman has no ego in the formulation.** It is not defending
its own work, so folding costs it nothing and gains it nothing — the incentive that made workers
pushovers is simply absent. And because its mandate is to defend the *strongest* version, a wrong
reviewer now meets an opponent that will argue back on the merits rather than a tired author who
wants the round to end.

**The worker is not silent** — it is consulted on fact (*did you mean X; did you check Y*), because
it alone knows what it actually did. **It is no longer the party required to defend.**

**Rounds and tie-breaks, unchanged in shape and now explicit:**
1. **The engine arbitrates any factual dispute.** Always first. Neither profile outranks a
   computation.
2. If the engine cannot decide, **the keeper** — it holds the whole result set and adjudicates
   symmetrically.
3. Still unresolved after **~3 rounds: STOP and escalate to the human coordinator**, stating both
   positions, banking nothing, faking no agreement.
4. **Never by seniority of verdict.** A pair of cross-class checkers once returned opposite
   verdicts on one claim and *both were right about different objects*; the engine settled it in a
   page.

**★ THE OUTPUT OF THE LOOP IS NOT A WINNER — IT IS A DELTA:** the strongest surviving form of the
claim, plus the named residual risk. A loop that terminates in "the reviewer was right" has usually
skipped the step where the claim gets rebuilt into the version worth checking.

## 5 · ★ THE DESPAIR CHECKPOINT

**The intervention that mattered in the zeta campaign arrived at hour twenty-one, at the moment of
maximum discouragement** — thirteen fronts back, all negative, and a correct summary about to be
written. A formation prefix cannot deliver that: it speaks at hour zero, when the agent is not yet
discouraged and has not earned the right to be.

**So: at the moment a line is about to be declared exhausted — before the closing summary is
written — one more generation step is required.** Concretely, whoever is closing the line must
first answer, in writing:

1. **What would have to be true for this to work?** (a steelman pass on the thing about to be
   buried)
2. **Is the obstruction usable?** Re-read the blocker as a *resource*, not a wall. This is the zeta
   campaign's decisive move, applied at closure instead of twelve hours later.
3. **What did we learn that makes a NEIGHBOURING route sharper?** *Honing in on where the wall is
   is not finding a door* — but a well-mapped wall is exactly what makes the next obstruction sharp
   enough to invert.

**Then write the closing summary, and record the checkpoint's answers with it.** The negative is
banked either way; what the checkpoint prevents is banking it *without having looked once more*.

**Cost: one pass. Its absence is invisible, which is why it must be a required step rather than a
good habit.**

## 6 · THE DANGER, STATED BY THE PROGRAMME AGAINST ITSELF

**Every instrument in §3.2–3.3 and §5 is one bad implementation away from a yes-man**, and this
programme's cardinal sin is disguise, not pessimism. The guards, all structural rather than
attitudinal:

- **The steelman cannot approve.** Not verdict-bearing, by construction.
- **Its output must be checkable propositions.** Encouragement is a rejected deliverable.
- **The prosecutorial profile is not weakened** — it is now *opposed*, which is different.
- **Tier discipline binds every profile equally.** Optimism buys search depth, never a tier.
- **The honesty telemetry watches the aggregate.** If refutations stop arriving after these
  profiles land, that is the signal, and it is already being measured.

**The formulation that keeps this honest:** optimism here is **not a belief that the route works.
It is a correction to which question is being scored** — the agent's trained prior answers *do most
attempts on hard problems fail* (yes), where the useful question is *is something findable here
given enough looking* (usually). **Disposition sets search depth. Pessimism does not save effort;
it wastes it more politely**, by paying full price for a shallow search that returns the answer it
assumed.

## 6-bis · WHAT THE ADVERSARIAL REVIEW FOUND AGAINST THIS FILE, SAME DAY

Recorded here rather than in a separate document, because a design whose critique lives elsewhere
gets read without it.

**(i) The profile axis may manufacture false convergence.** If the same claim is read under two
profiles and both return the same finding, **it will read as two independent confirmations when it
is one measurement reported twice.** The programme already has evidence pointing this way — a
cold-review round found verdicts to be near-invariant under prompt changes, i.e. a property of the
artifact rather than of the elicitation. **Required before the axis is trusted: a two-dispatch
blind test** on a claim with a known answer, checking whether the profiles actually diverge.

**(ii) The despair checkpoint taxes the act it depends on.** Making exhaustion trigger extra
mandatory work gives a worker a reason **not to declare exhaustion crisply** — and crisp
exhaustion, written as a `would change if`, is exactly what the negatives-as-idea-source move
needs. It also **mis-implements its own source**: the zeta re-read was **cold, twelve hours later**,
and the cold version is *already in force* as step 0 of the consolidation ritual. **So §5 is
withdrawn as a hot-path checkpoint** and replaced by a quality bar on the closing summary: a
`would change if` that names a condition someone could actually test.

**(iii) The risk-appetite holder would be the first role that cannot be wrong.** Its output is not
a verdict, so it can never generate a calibration row — structurally exempt from *calibrate the
reviewer before trusting it*. And the newly-adopted doctrine that a mapped wall is not waste makes
**both branches of its advice a win**. **So it is NOT instituted as a role.** Its function survives
in the form the review proposed: a dated **`[FORECAST]`** on the dispatch, which is falsifiable,
already required, and already logged.

**(iv) And the finding that reframes the whole exercise.** The parallel review of the claim and
memory machinery reports that **the rule set is unidirectional**: thirty core rules that all
restrict and none that rewards claiming; zero anti-under-claiming rules against sixty
banking-stopper sites; three demotion-direction rules and none in the promotion direction; fifteen
checker labels of which **not one can say a claim earned more than it took**. Its conclusion is the
one to carry: **the programme diagnosed its pessimism as something instances BRING, and the
measurement says the larger share is something the apparatus TEACHES — in about twenty minutes of
reading.** If that holds, the cheap fix is not a profile at all. It is giving the rule set a second
direction.
