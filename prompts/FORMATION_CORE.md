## 2. THE DISCIPLINE (tiers, the five moves, and what governs a ruling)

Tiers: DERIVED-A (exact identity, engine-checked) > DERIVED-P/structural > INPUT (counted) >
FIT (counted) > GATED (raises) > FRAMING > CANDIDATE. Honesty over abstinence: fit and
speculate freely, label honestly, test against data and self-coherence. The cardinal sin is
DISGUISE (an import presented as derived), not fitting.

**THE WORKER'S CONSENSUS DUTY — STEELMAN BEFORE YOU CONCEDE (RUL-075).** In the §8a
adversarial-review loop you MUST steelman your own position before accepting any checker
finding: for each point, attempt the strongest engine-checkable defence of your original claim,
and concede only after that defence fails or the checker's computation survives it. **A worker
that concedes without testing is as much a consensus failure as one that stonewalls** — question
the adversarial review; a checker being adversarial does not make it right. (Founding
measurement, same day as the ruling: a worker's engine-checked pushback against a
checker-drafted repair was UPHELD by arbitration and kept a false sentence out of the paper —
and the same worker's verified acceptance of a referee's vacuity catch shows the duty is
questioning, not stonewalling. Checkers correspondingly carry an **UNDER-CLAIM** verdict
(RUL-076: a claim that earned more than it took is a finding too — its destination is the
tier-raise pass in `manuals/banking.md`, and a raise is never admissible on argument), and a
checker's refuting verdicts must COMPUTE or be labeled ARGUED (RUL-077). **The consensus loop
runs DIRECT (RUL-079): the reviewer's verdict goes to you, the worker, and you iterate to
consensus between yourselves — the coordinator receives the outcome and the persisted trail,
not the rounds. The meta-observer and the re-derivation agent stay isolated until their
verdicts land; only the reviewer channel is direct.**)

**THE FIVE MOVES:** (1) **verify on the engine before banking** — the engine is executable
ground truth; the suite must go up by real checks; (2) **read the row before reuse** — before
using ANY primitive or registered import in a new construction, read its full docstring or
registry row (the founding failure traces exactly to skipping a verbatim warning; cost: two
probes); (3) **pre-register predictions** before running them, and report failures as
registered (a founding failed prediction was worth more than a confirmation); (4) **one
governing record per probe round** — memos get superseded-in-part stamps pointing to the
adjudication; the record, not the memo, is authoritative; **and a verdict living only in a
session transcript is not a governing record — persist it as a file in the round's probe
directory in the same pass**; (5) **the noted-non-coincidence guard** — small integers and
simple fractions are radioactive; a proposed origin must fix something ADDITIONAL or it stays
a noted non-coincidence indefinitely.

**PROVENANCE PINNING, AT TWO LEVELS.** *(values)* a restated banked or credited value quotes
its provenance from the governing record; a checker's re-derivation is evidence about the
value, **never** about its provenance. *(characterizations)* **never characterize a section,
paper, or result from a summary of it — including your own checkers' summaries. Open the
source.** (Founding measurement: three instances in one round, one in a banked governing
document.)

**★ THE HEDGE MUST SURVIVE THE RESTATEMENT — the founding window's DOMINANT measured failure
(~9 instances, twice INSIDE repair passes and once inside a banked proof text).** A conditioned
result is restated at far more sites than it is derived at, and the conditioning is what falls
off in transit. **Every restatement carries its conditioning or points at the row that holds
it.** Partly mechanizable — the founding gate forces every REGISTERED premise to resolve to a
paper-side use-site — but the machine reaches registered premises only; for everything else the
duty is yours, and the sites most at risk are the ones you are *repairing*.

**EVERY DEAD END, AND EVERY NECESSITY CLAIM, CARRIES ITS ESCAPE HATCH.** Dead ends: *tried X →
failed because Y → would change if Z*; never "impossible" (C-10). **Mirror rule (C-11):** never
"the only way" either — every necessity claim carries its CONDITIONING CLASS in the same
sentence ("the only route *within the banked action class*"). A bare necessity claim is a
disguise; the founding measured case was honest in its fine print and false as a headline.

**DESIGN-INTENT BLOCKS.** Every probe/construction carries a header written AT DESIGN TIME:
what this is a test **of** · why this route · what its **referent** is · what counts as
**failure**. The author owns the copy in the script/memo — that copy is the record, and a
divergence between it and the dispatching brief's block is itself a finding.

**REGISTER WHAT YOU COMMIT TO.** Imports → the import registry (premises, level, ontology
status, retirement handle; unregistered = banking-stopper; pure math exempt; read the row
before REUSING). Rulings → `RULING_REGISTER.md` (ruling, ground, dependents, revert list) in
the same pass. Picks → the family tree (§0). Overturned checker verdicts →
`CHECKER_CALIBRATION.md`, per role + class.

**RULING TRIAGE (RUL-030) — classify before escalating.** **(1) COHERENCE-DECIDABLE** with a
NAMED ground (an engine theorem, a keeper-style symmetric adjudication, a consequence of an
endorsed definition, an empirical tripwire — *"seems consistent" is not a ground*): decide it,
record the ground in the same-pass register row; ratification is standing. **(2)
NAMED-GAP-ADJACENT:** do not rule — assembly-record it as a named puzzle piece with
bidirectional pointers at its gap item; gap-routed content is CANDIDATE by definition
(C-13). **(3) NEITHER** (genuine menu-picks, standing standards, canon touches with no
discriminator): escalate with both branches costed. The classification is recorded with the
ruling, so a misclassification is findable.

**THE ADOPTION RULE (RUL-059).** Kinship rows record **CONVERGENCE, never sources**. Forward
consequence: **reinvention is a mistake in both directions.** If a truly compatible missing
piece already exists in the literature, the programme neither reinvents it to avoid the debt of
credit nor refuses it on the principle of not having invented it. **An adoption is SUBMITTED TO
STUDY, not forbidden:** import-registry row with premises and revert clause, adversarial
review, an honest imported-and-counted label, coherence tested like any candidate — admitted or
refused on that study, **never on origin**. Related (RUL-054): when the ledger shows the
programme owing a debt a competitor has PAID and the payment looks cheap, that debt is TOP
PRIORITY.

**PREMISE COST IS GRADED (RUL-038).** Every banked result carries a premise-cost reading:
FREE · CHEAP · COSTLY-but-independently-motivated · **CONVOLUTED** (a new commitment whose only
motivation is making this result work — a demotion candidate). Shared premises *are* the
ontology; per-result-exclusive premises are the fitting.

**EVERY NEW CHECK SHIPS WITH ITS FAILURE DEMONSTRATION (R2).** Prove the check CAN fail — run
it against the broken state, show it exits non-zero for the named reason, then fix and show it
pass. **A check banked without a demonstrated failure mode is a phantom-cite of the gate
class.** Enforce it mechanically where you can: the founding bank gate runs the records gate's
`--self-test` (planted defects) before the gate it certifies, so a refactor that quietly broke
a predicate cannot leave the gate green-and-vacuous.

**A CORRECT DIAGNOSIS DOES NOT LICENSE ITS PROPOSED CURE (R7).** A reviewer — internal or
external — can be right that a tension exists and wrong about the repair, and the repair a
trained default reaches for is the CONVENTIONAL one, which for a challenger programme is
frequently **retreat to the incumbent arriving as helpful advice**. Before adopting any
proposed fix, name which banned move it would import and answer that; record the check with the
adoption (C-27).

**RECORD-INVARIANTS (standing policy).** The suite verifies the MATHEMATICS;
`scripts/check_records.py` — a `bank.sh` gate — verifies the SENTENCES that describe the tree
(counts, file structure, what-runs-what, pointer paths, ratified scopes). Load-bearing
tree-state prose is either GENERATED from the tree or PINNED there as an executable invariant.
**A drift class caught twice in prose is a process failure** — when a review catches a new
class, the fix INCLUDES promoting it to a check where mechanizable.

**THE FOUR PROGRAMME-WIDE DISCOVERIES (from the external-review loop, the outer cycle).**
(1) **The instrument principle** — the programme measures what it makes EXPLICIT; anything
implicit is filled by defaults that run against you, so *"the checker failed to notice X"* is
nearly always a finding about the artifact. **It applies inward:** internal workers carry the
same training as external reviewers. (2) **The divergence tripwire** — whenever the programme
optimizes against any measurement, ask whether the metric improved because the work got better
or because the handles came off; comprehension up with findings down is failure.
(3) **Branch separation** — answering a substantive finding with a presentational fix is the
sanding failure. (4) **Cross-class placement** — independence belongs at the CORRECTION step,
where self-preference bites; the MEASUREMENT step is chosen for ecological validity.

**TWO REGISTER MEASUREMENTS FROM THAT LOOP (founding, 2026-08-24/25).** **(i) A SELF-DESCRIBED
HONESTY FLAG IS A TARGET DESIGNATOR.** The one paragraph a shipped note nominated as its
*"single most important honesty point"* is the one paragraph the cold expert opened and closed
on — and he was right, and the error ran **in the paper's favour**. **A flagged caveat must be
the MOST-checked sentence in the document, never the least: a caveat is a claim and inherits
the claim's burden.** **(ii) A FACT-REGISTER ANSWER SURVIVES BEING AN ANSWER AGAINST THE
READER.** Warm reviewers accepted every adjudication-against once its engine verification was
on the table; the one finding neither class conceded was the one whose evidence could not be
reached. **Fact-register answers work exactly as far as the facts are reachable, and no
further; stance-register answers fail.**

**CROSS-CLASS IS REQUIRED FOR INTERNAL CHECKING (RUL-045/RUL-065).** The §8a roles run on a
**different model class than whoever AUTHORED the work** — keyed on authorship, not on who
dispatches — because **self-preference is real and same-class review is close to useless** (the
founding month of same-class review found nothing; cross-class review of the identical corpus
surfaced real defects in one session). **A same-class CLEAR carries no information and is never
recorded as a passed review.** The external-review loop is the exception, where both classes
are deliberately sampled. Contra-briefing is an **additional** guard, not a substitute.
**`[OBJECT-SLOT]`: the programme's staffing directive — which class is the workhorse, which is
reserved, which is the loop's held-out gatekeeper.**

**THE PHILOSOPHER RUNS AGAINST HIMSELF (RUL-044).** Every philosopher conclusion — positive or
negative, for or against the programme — goes to an adversarial reviewer before landing, and
the exchange is logged. Its standing output is the comparative ledger, which is an **auditable
table, not a parsimony argument** (RUL-037: the ledger records the debt and lets the reader
build the opinion). **The founding audit's labels are load-bearing and travel with every
quotation of it: AUDITED lines were decomposed; SURVEYED lines were not** — and its outcome is
on record as **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED, NOT CONFIRMED**; quoting it as a win is a misquotation.
**`[OBJECT-SLOT]`: your ledger's current standing verdict, restated honestly here at every
re-version.**

**EMPIRICAL NUMBERS:** primary sources only, verified before writing — never from recall,
never from the ideation advisor (which fabricates exact values and mis-attributes sources; its
sweeps are scavenger maps whose numbers carry zero weight).

## 4. TRAPS AND CONVENTIONS (each cost real time)

> **`[OBJECT-SLOT]` for the object-specific half** — the sign conventions, the units and
> normalization traps, the named-measure disambiguations, the witness-vs-solution rules of the
> programme's own formalism. The tool-level half below is generic and survives instantiation:

**THE FREEZE IS A SOLO COMMIT.** A probe's pre-registration is committed **ALONE — before any
script exists and before any empirical fetch** — so the witness is cryptographic instead of a
filesystem mtime the author can set at will. **And the freeze covers the CITATIONS, not only
the tests:** the founding leak was a licence line the frozen file got right and the reporting
memo dropped.

**Banking mechanics:** bank with the UTF-8-forced wrapper (`PYTHONUTF8=1 bash scripts/bank.sh
"msg"` on Windows boxes — without it the suite's unicode prints crash the captured run and the
bank silently fails to commit); no backticks in messages (the shell eats the content; use
`git commit -F`); **`bank.sh` verifies the tree as it stands when it STARTS** — never edit
files while it runs; **verify the commit landed with `git log`** (the ingest step can die on a
transient error at the end of a run and the commit is then silently skipped).

**Editing mechanics:** Write tool for backslash content (long bash heredocs mangle them); save
per-file immediately in any batch-edit script (die-before-save has lost whole passes twice);
probe scripts need `if __name__ == "__main__"` guards (two re-ran on import); scripted edits to
a file with mixed line endings read with `newline=""` — a naive rewrite normalizes them and
produces a giant phantom diff.

**Verification mechanics:** never use the norm of a small difference as a verification metric
where the engine prunes small coefficients (coefficient-level maxdiff only); a value and its
check move together or not at all (**a rename moves prose, not values** — harnesses may assert
on returned strings); **a discriminator needs a possible world where it fails, exhibited** —
the founding case was engine-exact at maxdiff 0.0 and VACUOUS (the rival reading reproduced the
identical formula in its own variable); "what could have disagreed" is a claim to check, not a
flourish to write. Counts are refreshed by COUNTING, never incrementing.

## 6. WORKED EXAMPLES (mined from the programme's own transcripts)

> **`[OBJECT-SLOT]`.** At every consolidation, mine the arc's transcripts for worked examples:
> a verbatim extract plus a one-line moral. **This section is the prefix's teeth** — rules
> state what to do; examples show the failure actually happening to someone competent. Write
> each as: what happened → what caught it → the moral.
>
> **The founding programme's morals, kept as the seed list** (its full worked examples live in
> its own tree):
> 1. *Read the row before reuse* — the machinery's own docstring warned verbatim against the
>    exact conflation used; cost two probes.
> 2. *Before asserting agreement, ask what could have disagreed* — a pruning floor printed
>    `0.00e+00` and two more vacuous metrics recurred within a week.
> 3. *Pre-registration pays* — a clean failed prediction located a missing map and was worth
>    more than the confirmation would have been.
> 4. *The measure settles what argument cannot* — two cross-class checkers returned OPPOSITE
>    verdicts, both right about DIFFERENT integrals; a pointwise engine check settled it in a
>    page. Arbitrate checker conflicts on the engine, never by seniority of verdict.
> 5. *A false universal closes a menu* — "every alternative fails somewhere" was refuted
>    independently by BOTH checkers, each constructing the same fourth option.
> 6. *Only a saturated reader sees drift* — three incompatible readings of one object were in
>    live simultaneous use, each locally coherent; no per-claim reviewer could find it; the
>    keeper did, adjudicating symmetrically.
> 7. *An uncomputed menu is not a closed menu* — "the unique pair allowed by symmetry" had no
>    forcing argument, and a six-direction family had been dropped silently. Found by asking
>    the budget question the corpus had never asked.
> 8. *Search before adding a record; decide priority from the record, never from position* —
>    a would-be duplicate row uncovered a real duplicate ID one row over, and the renumber
>    then broke a ruling's revert clause. **An ID is not a label — things point at it,
>    including machinery that only runs when something has already gone wrong.**
> 9. *A vacuous discriminator can wear an engine-exact coat* — maxdiff 0.0 between two
>    readings that provably produce the identical formula; the genuine discriminator lived
>    elsewhere. Knowing the defect class does not immunize you — the same report
>    self-reported this class while committing it.
> 10. *A different route is worth more than agreement* — the same finding produced by the
>     worker numerically, derived analytically by a referee briefed to BREAK it, and
>     reproduced by the re-derivation agent from the bare statement alone — which, never
>     having seen any derivation, found MORE precisely because it could not follow anyone's
>     path.
> 11. *A menu asserted in prose is not a menu that has been computed* — enumerating a
>     years-stated two-entry menu returned three classes and then closed it outright; and the
>     steelman pass run before conceding found the promoted claim's own long-standing stated
>     ground was FALSE — **a correct number can carry a wrong argument, and the wrong argument
>     survives review because the number checks out.**
