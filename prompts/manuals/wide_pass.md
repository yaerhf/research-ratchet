# MANUAL · THE WIDE PASS — borrow a formalism from a domain that is not yours

**Trigger: a line is stuck, or a route is about to be chosen.** *Range before you dig deeper*
(C-34). Complete for the activity: read this and you need nothing else to run one.

> **Why this exists, and it is a measured observation rather than a theory.** A programme running a
> second provider's model as an in-apparatus generator reported the gap precisely: **the knowledge
> was never the problem.** When the other model named a door, this class recognised it at once and
> could verify and use it. What differed was **posture** — *"le nez dans le guidon"* against *"la
> tête dans les nuages"* (human coordinator, 2026-09-23). **A posture is a diet, and a diet is a
> role.** So the wide pass is not a better prompt for the same dispatch; it is a DIFFERENT dispatch,
> starved of everything that invites depth, whose product is handed to an ordinary one.

---

## 1 · THE DIET — enforced, not requested

`python rag/diet.py --role wide FILE` answers before anything is opened. The wide worker is
**starved of**: the formation prefix and the corpus (*the programme's own picture is exactly what
the pass exists to leave*), the current derivation, the engine, persisted verdicts, session
transcripts, governing adjudications, **and every checker's role definition** — that last one so it
**cannot learn to anticipate the checkers**, which is a worker taught to please them, and the
measurement destroyed from the inside.

**It receives three things and no more:** its brief · **the problem stated in plain words, stripped
of the programme's formalism** · the negatives index, so a located dead end does not come back.

**The hole in every starvation is the brief itself** — a derivation quoted inside a brief is served,
because a diet bounds files and not what somebody pastes. Pinned as a blind spot in
`rag/diet.py --self-test`. **Whoever writes the brief carries this one.**

---

## 2 · STEP 0 — THE SKELETON, BEFORE ANY DOMAIN IS NAMED

**The pass opens by abstracting the problem, and the abstraction is an ARTIFACT, not a posture.**
*(Human coordinator, 2026-09-23, asking whether phrases like "take the high ground" or "abstract
the essence" belong in the brief. The intent is right and it was the missing step: without it the
search matches on SUBJECT — the problem mentions light, so bring optics — which is the surface
matching the whole pass exists to avoid.)*

**Five lines, no domain nouns, before anything else:** the objects · the relations between them ·
what is conserved and what is free · the boundary or the limit · **and the question's TYPE** —
existence, uniqueness, stability, selection, transport, scaling, counting. **Then every candidate
must say which line of the skeleton its equations match.** A door that matches no line is a door
into another problem.

**★ AND THE SKELETON CARRIES ITS OWN OMISSIONS.** Abstraction is where a problem gets lost: drop
the feature that makes it hard and every door afterwards answers an easier question, convincingly.
So the skeleton ends with **the two or three features it deliberately dropped**, and the second
pass checks each survivor against them — *does the dropped feature kill this door?* That is the
blind-spot discipline turned on the abstraction itself.

**Why the step and not the phrase.** *"Take the high ground"* is a posture instruction: pleasant,
unfalsifiable, and it buys grand vagueness as easily as insight — *"this is really a problem about
information"* is what the high ground sounds like when it fails. The structural form of the same
intent is what this section asks for: **name the question type, name the invariant, and say what
you dropped.** A vivid phrase may LABEL a step; it cannot BE one.

---

## 3 · THE ASK — doors, not analogies

**3 to 10 candidates, each from a domain whose behaviour is governed by equations**, and the point
of that restriction is mechanical rather than aesthetic: **a formalism can be transplanted and
TESTED; a resemblance cannot.** *(Human coordinator, 2026-09-23, from watching which of the other
model's doors actually paid: they were always mathematically modellable domains.)*

**★ DISTINCT EQUATION FAMILIES, NOT DISTINCT SUBJECTS. Two domains that share a governing form are
ONE door.** Ten analogies that all reduce to diffusion are one door with ten costumes, and a wide
pass that returns them has failed even when every candidate is individually sound. Spread across
forms — conservation and continuity · diffusion and parabolic relaxation · wave and hyperbolic
propagation · variational and least-action · spectral and eigenvalue · stochastic (Langevin, master
equation, jump process) · combinatorial and graph-spectral · information-theoretic · control with
feedback and delay · optimisation and duality · thermodynamic and entropic · scaling and
renormalisation. **That list is a starting spread, never a menu to fill.**

**Each candidate carries, in this order, and nothing else:**

1. **The domain**, named.
2. **Its governing equation(s) in their OWN symbols** — the home domain's notation, not the
   target's.
3. **The mapping**: one line per symbol — what each becomes in the problem at hand.
4. **★ DIMENSIONAL COHERENCE, stated.** Does the mapping balance dimensionally? If it does not, say
   so and say what would have to carry the missing dimension. **This is the first gate and it is
   nearly free** — a decorative analogy dies here, in one line, before anybody spends a dispatch
   on it.
5. **What the mapped equation predicts**, in one sentence.
6. **The kill world** — the case where the mapped equation must give the known-wrong answer
   (C-33 / the checking manual §0-bis). A door with no kill world is not yet a door.
7. **What it does not claim.**

**Forbidden in this pass:** the target's own vocabulary and formalism (they pull the search home) ·
verification · derivation · elaboration beyond the lines above. **And say it in the brief:** *you
will not be judged on whether any of these is right.* An instruction to be creative is worth less
than a structure in which caution has nothing to do.

**Run it k times, independently, not once at length.** Breadth comes from sampling, not from asking
harder: k short generations and a triage beat one long answer, every time.

---

## 4 · THE SECOND PASS — where the depth is allowed back in

The door list is the artifact between the two diets, and it is what makes this auditable rather
than a mood. **An ordinary dispatch then takes the surviving doors and does what this class does
well:** write the mapped equation in the target's own variables and **run it through the
instrument's own pipeline** — **the kill world first, the real case only after it behaves**
(C-20-bis), with a planted failure showing the check can fail.

**★ AND EACH SURVIVOR IS CHECKED AGAINST THE SKELETON'S OMISSIONS** (§2): *is one of the dropped
features what kills this door?* **A door that works only on the abstracted problem is a door into
the easier problem**, and it will read exactly like a door into this one.

**Every door is CANDIDATE** until it has done that, and often after: **a formalism that maps is not
a mechanism that is true.** The tier is not a judgement of worth — in the programme this manual was
written from, the best work entered that way.

---

## 5 · HOW YOU KNOW THE PASS WORKED

Not by how it reads. Count:

- **distinct equation families** returned (not domains);
- **doors that survive the second pass** — the mapped equation behaving on the kill world;
- **doors absent from the negatives index** — a live one, not a dead end returning in costume.

**A pass that returns only families the programme already uses has failed**, and that failure is
worth recording: it prices what the outside instrument is buying (W23).

## 6 · THE BRIEF, READY TO SEND

*Fill the three slots, send it to k independent instances, triage the union. Nothing else is added
— every line below is load-bearing, and the omissions are deliberate.*

```text
You are running a WIDE PASS. You are a generator, not a solver, and you are not being asked
to be right.

THE PROBLEM, in plain words:
  [PROBLEM — stated WITHOUT this programme's formalism, notation or preferred route]

DEAD ENDS ALREADY LOCATED (do not return these, in any costume):
  [the negatives index, or its rows near this problem]

FIRST, THE SKELETON — before you name a single domain. Five lines, using NO domain nouns:
  - the objects;
  - the relations between them;
  - what is conserved, and what is free;
  - the boundary or the limit;
  - the QUESTION TYPE: existence / uniqueness / stability / selection / transport / scaling /
    counting.
  Then one more line: THE TWO OR THREE FEATURES YOU DROPPED to write those five. Abstraction is
  where a problem gets lost, and a door that answers the easier problem reads exactly like a
  door that answers this one.

THEN: between 3 and 10 candidates. Each candidate borrows the GOVERNING EQUATIONS of a domain
that is not this one, and each must say WHICH LINE OF THE SKELETON its equations match. More is
better, on one condition:

  ★ DISTINCT EQUATION FAMILIES, NOT DISTINCT SUBJECTS. Two domains that share a governing form
    count as ONE candidate. Ten analogies that all reduce to diffusion are one door in ten
    costumes. Spread across forms: conservation, diffusion, wave, variational, spectral,
    stochastic, combinatorial, information-theoretic, control-with-delay, optimisation,
    thermodynamic, scaling. That is a spread to aim past, not a menu to fill.

EACH CANDIDATE, IN THIS ORDER, AND NOTHING ELSE:
  0. Which skeleton line it matches.
  1. Domain.
  2. Its governing equation(s) IN THEIR OWN SYMBOLS — the home domain's notation.
  3. The mapping: one line per symbol, what each becomes in the problem above.
  4. Dimensional coherence: does the mapping balance? If not, say what would have to carry the
     missing dimension. (Answer this in one line. A candidate that cannot is already dead.)
  5. What the mapped equation predicts — one sentence.
  6. The kill world: the case where this mapped equation MUST give the known-wrong answer.
  7. What it does not claim.

DO NOT: verify, derive, simulate, or elaborate. Do not use this programme's vocabulary — state
each candidate in its home domain's language and let the mapping do the translation. Do not
rank them. Do not exceed the seven lines per candidate.

YOU WILL NOT BE JUDGED ON WHETHER ANY OF THESE IS RIGHT. A candidate that dies in one line at
step 4 has done its job. A list that is safe and familiar has not.
```

**Then, and only then, a separate ordinary dispatch verifies the survivors** — mapped equation in
the target's own variables, kill world first, real case after (§4).

---

## 7 · WHAT THIS MANUAL IS NOT FOR

A door that **cannot be written as an equation in the target's variables** is not a door for this
pass. It may still be a good idea, and it goes to the docket as one — but it does not get to borrow
this pass's authority, because the whole authority here comes from being testable by transplant.
