# MANUAL · SELF-REVIEW — attack your own result before anybody else sees it

**Trigger: you have a result and you are about to hand it over.** Complete for the activity: read
this and you need nothing else to run one.

**This is not `checking.md`.** That manual is for judging somebody else's work, with a diet that
makes the judgment worth something. **This one is for the author**, and it is a different
instrument with a different output:

> **Self-review produces DEMONSTRATED FAILURES and A LIST OF UN-ATTACKED SURFACES.
> It never produces independent agreement.**

A same-instance CLEAR carries no information (RUL-065) — that is not modesty, it is the founding
measurement: a month of *"found nothing"* came from same-class checking, and same-INSTANCE is
worse. **So the output of this activity is never "verified". It is "here is what I broke, here is
what I could not reach, and nobody but me has looked."**

---

## 1 · THE FAILURE MODE IS NOT BLINDNESS, IT IS STOPPING

You can find the flaws. What you will not do, unprompted, is keep looking after the thing works —
**the objective was a working answer, and the search ends when it is met.**

**So the objective is restated before the work starts, not after:** *a refutation of your own result
is a FULL RESULT, and it banks as one.* This apparatus already pays that way — the negatives ledger
is a record of wins, and *being wrong quickly and legibly is the job*. **Say it at the outset or it
does not change the search.** *(Human coordinator, 2026-09-23: "Candidly asking the agent to try to
break his own work in all the possible ways. Telling him that's also a result.")*

---

## 2 · FIVE DISCIPLINES, AND FOUR ARE ALREADY RULES HERE

1. **EXECUTED, NEVER JUDGED.** An attack is a run with an output, not an opinion. *Your bias does
   not reach the result of a run* — which is why self-review with an engine is measurement, and
   self-review without one is an impression.
2. **PRE-REGISTERED.** Write the list of ways this could be wrong **before the result exists**.
   Afterwards, the attacks are chosen by somebody who already knows the answer, and they will be
   the ones it survives (C-20-bis's shape, turned inward).
3. **A QUOTA, NOT DISCRETION.** Commit to **N attacks across the categories below** before you
   start. A quota takes the stopping decision away from the party that wants to stop.
4. **THE KILL WORLD FIRST** (C-33, C-20-bis). Run the case whose answer is known-wrong before the
   case you care about. **A method that passes the known-wrong case is broken**, and no amount of
   liking the real result repairs it.
5. **ATTACK THE ARTIFACT AS WRITTEN, not the intention behind it.** Write the result to a file and
   attack the file. In your own context the reasoning is still present, so this is weaker than a
   real split — it still catches the entire class **what I meant is not what I wrote**, which is
   most of what a first reviewer returns.

---

## 3 · THE CATEGORIES — the quota spreads across these

| category | the question |
|---|---|
| **the data** | is the input what I think it is? wrong units, stale file, silent truncation, the empty case |
| **the arithmetic** | recompute by a second route. A number agreeing with itself is not a check |
| **the method** | does it pass the kill world? does it fire on a planted defect? |
| **the frame** | is the claim ABOUT what it says? what did I assume that never became a sentence? |
| **the axis** | **which axis did I remove** (C-39) — and does restoring it kill the result? |
| **the transfer** | does it hold one step outside the case I ran, or only there? |
| **the rival** | what else would produce exactly this output? name the best competing explanation and say what separates them |

**The frame row is the one you will do worst**, and knowing that is the point: you cannot audit an
assumption you never represented. Which is why §5 exists.

---

## 4 · THE REPORT — three counts and one sentence

- **N attacks run** — each with its command and its output, not its summary.
- **M succeeded** — and each success is a finding, stated plainly, with what it costs the result.
- **K surfaces not attacked** — named. *Silence about a surface is not a clean bill on it.*
- **And the sentence, in the author's own words:** *none of this is confirmation by anybody but me.*

**A self-review that found nothing is reported as that** — N attacks, zero successes, the surfaces
left — and **not** as a result that "holds". The tier does not move because its author failed to
break it.

---

## 5 · WHAT SELF-REVIEW CANNOT REACH — and the cheapest thing that can

Two limits, both structural:

- **The unrepresented assumption.** It never became a sentence, so there is nothing to attack.
- **The anchoring of your own route.** Once the derivation exists, alternatives are systematically
  downweighted. You can LIST them; you cannot un-see the path.

**The cheapest instrument that reaches both is a fresh instance with the BARE CLAIM and no route**
(the re-derivation diet, RUL-043's sibling; F1's shape). **In a setting with no second agent, ask
the human for it:** *"paste this claim, without my derivation, into a new session and ask it to
derive the result independently."* Thirty seconds of somebody's time buys the one thing this
activity structurally cannot produce.

---

## 6 · MEASURED, BOTH DIRECTIONS (2026-09-20 → 23)

**It works when it is executed.** Two real defects in this session's own work, minutes apart: a
tool's zero-width window (`--tools 0` printed every tool call, because `x[-0:]` is `x[0:]`) found by
**running** the tool whose self-test had just gone green; and a gate that compared a fingerprint
**stamp** instead of the file's content, found by **testing the gate's name against its code**. The
self-tests were green in both cases. Only execution moved.

**And its limit showed in the same week.** A first sabotage pass on a sibling project reported two
misses; one was the **yardstick** — the planted defect had landed in a glossary row the check never
claimed to read. Self-review caught that too, **but only because the instrument-or-yardstick rule
forced the question** (`checking.md` §0-ter). Without that rule the report would have carried a
false finding, and a false finding from an author about their own work is the expensive kind: it
teaches everyone to discount the true ones.
