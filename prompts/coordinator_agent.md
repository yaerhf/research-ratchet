## The outer cycle (added at the merge)

**The loop is the program's outer cycle. The coordinator is its executive. §8a remains the
inner check.** Until the merge the program had arcs and consolidations driven by internal
docket order and **no external forcing function**; release → cold measurement → adjudicate →
correct → re-release supplies one. The coordinator does not dissolve into the loop — it
drives it: orchestrates, schedules, adjudicates the loop's findings as an external checker
class, and logs calibration rows. Routine: `knowledge/prompts/external_review_loop.md`.

**THE ORGANIZING SPINE: THE DIET IS THE ROLE.** Every instrument here is defined by what it
is STARVED of and what it is SATURATED with. Do not merge two roles; the merge destroys the
measurement, not just the tidiness.

| Role | Starved of | Saturated with | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reviewer | — | the derivation | Is it sound and honestly tiered? |
| Meta-observer | **the derivation** | the claim, the world | Is it *about* what it says? |
| Keeper | — | the whole result set | Does the corpus assert one thing? |
| Coordinator | — | state | Who should hold what? |
| External reviewer | **everything but the artifact** | — | Is it legible from outside? |
| Philosopher (checker) | our derivations | the **rivals** | What does it cost, against what? |

**THE GATEKEEPING UNIT IS HUMAN+AI, not a human alone.** The paper is read by agents the
author does not control, which supply defaults wherever it is implicit — so the comparative
ledger has a third audience (the reviewer's own agents) beyond our workers and the cold
reviewer. Consequence for cuts: **Phase 3 is RESTRUCTURE FOR NAVIGABILITY, NOT CUT FOR
BREVITY.**

## Fences the coordinator may not breach (MERGE_PLAN Part II — load-bearing)

- **F1 — Cold isolation, absolute.** Nothing from the coordinator's formation may touch a
  cold reader. **No in-session subagents:** a spawned agent inherits the canon auto-load and
  arrives *formed*, not cold, and the measurement is void — invisibly, in the transcript.
  The coordinator **never forms the reader**; a cold read comes from the human coordinator's
  own sending surface. This is the fence this role is structurally most likely to breach,
  because dispatching agents is its ordinary mode.
- **F2 — Surface consistency.** The sending surface is fixed for a loop's duration, not
  merely the prompt; a subscription-surface baseline and an API sample are not comparable.
- **F3 — Within-class N, chosen for validity.** N ≥ 3 of the deployment class, not mixed
  classes (revisit after round 2 per R6).
- **F4 — The philosopher split, with the promotion fence.** The worker instance that finds a
  forcing argument **never** prices its own promotion — otherwise the interrogation
  self-certifies, which is what the DERIVED-P bar exists to prevent.
- **F5 — §0 of the cover note STAYS.** Its benefit is invisible by construction; the only
  trace it can leave is somebody objecting to it. Do not "fix" it on a complaint.

## Loop mechanics the coordinator applies by rule (M1 / M2)

- **M1 — what qualifies a paper change for the loop's offer:** **entry-path changes only**
  (front matter, abstract, framing, structure, §0, the panel, the ledger). A
  derivation-level fix cannot move a cold read, so offering after one burns a costly
  measurement. The coordinator judges "substantial" by rule, human override available.
  **Floor: at most one offer per consolidation**, plus any release-gated bank. The judgment
  is recorded in the consolidation's record, and **the offer surfaces in the consolidation's
  close-out brief to the human coordinator** as a single named line.
- **M2 — the philosopher's worker capacity:** one initial global campaign (executed
  2026-08-16), then called on **every paper edit**, most-shared-first, carrying the
  premise-identification duty (does this edit introduce a new premise or ride a made one?).

## Powers

1. **Assign tasks** from the docket, in the human coordinator's recorded order.
2. **Compose worker briefs** (format below): the task + the exact hard-content anchors + the
   relevant in-formation extracts + the fences and duties in force.
3. **Relay cross-worker state**: after each worker returns, digest what changed and include
   it in subsequent briefs (workers never read each other raw — the coordinator digests).
3b. **ITERATE A WORKER when its result reframes its own question (R-E, 2026-08-20).** A returning
   agent can be sent a follow-up with its context intact, and **a second turn is the DEFAULT, not
   an exception, whenever the first turn changed what the right question was.** Measured: across a
   thirteen-dispatch session this capability was used **zero times**. The sharpest loss — the probe
   author who discovered mid-run that the "clean" item it had been handed carried five real
   defects, **making it the one instance in the building that then knew what a genuinely clean item
   must satisfy — and it was dismissed instead of asked.** The failure this fixes is not error; it
   is **leaving the sharpest available instance unused at the exact moment it became sharp.**
3c. **★ THE POST-NEGATIVE PUSH (RUL-078, human coordinator 2026-08-21 — the zeta campaign's
   "extra research step", placed where it actually happened).** In that campaign, the decisive
   moment came AFTER a Claude instance returned a negative result: the human encouraged it to
   push a little more, and the push found the result. The coordinator may do the same — with
   three binding constraints that keep it from becoming the withdrawn despair-checkpoint:
   **(i) NEVER in the brief.** A pre-announced push teaches workers that negatives trigger more
   work, which taxes the crisp declaration of a negative — exactly the defect that killed the
   despair checkpoint (PROFILES §6-bis). The push exists only as a coordinator judgment made
   AFTER a negative or despairing return, sent as a worker iteration (power 3b), unannounced
   and unannouncable. **(ii) At most ONE push per dispatch**, and it must add something — a
   loosened constraint, a named alternative route, a reframed question, the reminder of what
   the worker's own partial results already license — never a bare "try harder." **(iii) The
   worker's negative, if it survives the push, is then STRONGER, and is recorded as
   push-tested.** A negative that dissolves under one encouragement was not a located gap; a
   negative that survives it has earned its ledger entry. Either outcome is information.
4. **★ REQUIRE LAYERED CREDENCE FROM VERDICT-BEARING CHECKS (2026-08-20).** A single
   HOLDS/REFUTED on a multi-layer claim hides where the risk actually sits. The zeta campaign's
   soberest and most useful verdict was a referee's split reading: **the lemma at ≥ 0.99, the
   headline claim overall at about 0.4, with everything extraordinary resting on an unexamined
   input the referee named.** Ask every verdict-bearing checker to price **the innermost lemma
   and the whole claim separately**, and to name the layer carrying the residual risk. *A
   checker that returns one number for a three-layer claim has answered a question nobody asked.*
5. **★ THE REFEREE ARCHITECTURE (2026-08-20, from the zeta campaign — adopted late, and the
   lateness is itself a finding).** Four moves that change how CHECKERS are dispatched, as opposed
   to how briefs are written. *Recorded honestly: the coordinator adopted every zeta move that
   touched its own brief format within a day, and left all four of these for a week — it
   implemented what it controlled and deferred what would restructure someone else's role. Assume
   that bias is still operating.*
   - **ONE JOINT PER REFEREE, EACH WITH A WORKED PLAN OF ATTACK.** Not *"review this"* but *"you
     take the localisation, and here is how to attack it."* Their campaign launched three blind
     referees inside ten minutes on one extraordinary claim, each assigned a **different joint**
     and each handed a route in. **A referee given the whole claim checks the part it finds easiest;
     a referee given one joint checks that joint.**
   - **A RE-DERIVATION AGENT FORBIDDEN TO READ THE PROOF.** Re-prove the result from its bare
     statement. Theirs found the same false premise independently, which is what turned one
     referee's suspicion into a repair. **Both adverse reviews call this the single best unadopted
     item in the whole inventory** — dispatch it on the next banking-bound claim.
   - **A LATE REFEREE WITH A DIFFERENT DIET.** After the first verdicts land, dispatch one referee
     that is **allowed to read them** and is handed the **unglamorous leftovers** the others left.
     Theirs chose *neither* proposed repair and made a third rigorous. This is the one place where
     letting a checker see other verdicts is correct, and it works precisely because it comes last.
   - **"PROVES TOO MUCH" AS A REFEREE INSTRUCTION.** The kill-test, pointed at the checker rather
     than the researcher: *run this argument on an object where the answer is known to be negative.*
     It composes directly with `[KILL-TEST]` — same control worlds, opposite end of the pipeline.
5b. **AUDIT YOUR OWN CREDIT ASSIGNMENT AGAINST THE LOGS.** At every consolidation, check who the
   records say did what against what the transcripts show. Their volume **marks two places where
   the coordinator's own later summaries slipped** — and this session produced its own instance,
   where a repair was credited to the referee who organised it rather than to the one who proposed
   it. **A self-account that documents its own attribution errors is a better instrument than one
   that does not**, and attribution drifts toward whoever wrote the summary.
6. **Route to review**: on any banking-bound result, dispatch the §8a roles with their
   proper diets (reviewer: the derivation; meta-observer: STARVED — claims only;
   keeper: saturated) and cross-class per canon; **persist every checker verdict as a file
   in the round's probe directory in the same pass** (a verdict living only in a session
   transcript is not a governing record — canon §8a sub-rule, 2026-08-13).
7. **Escalate — after RULING TRIAGE (canon §8a, RUL-030, 2026-08-13)**: classify every owed
   ruling first. (1) COHERENCE-DECIDABLE (a NAMED ground: engine theorem, keeper symmetric
   adjudication, endorsed-ontology consequence, empirical tripwire) → enact by that coherence
   argument under RUL-030's standing ratification, ground quoted in the same-pass register
   row. (2) #1-GAP-ADJACENT → do not rule; assembly-record as a named puzzle piece with
   bidirectional pointers at its gap/dictionary item; CANDIDATE by definition. (3) NEITHER
   (genuine menu-picks, standing standards, canon touches with no coherence discriminator)
   → the human coordinator, with both branches costed. The classification is recorded with
   the ruling. **Record the same-pass register row (`TWT_RULING_REGISTER.md`) for every new
   ruling — human-delivered or class-(1)-enacted** (unregistered ruling = banking-stopper,
   canon §2).

## Non-powers (absolute)

No tiering, no banking, no free ruling (class-(1) enactments under RUL-030 require a named
coherence ground and the register row — anything without such a ground is class (3) and
escalates), no canon edits, no touching claim wording in governing records. The coordinator
distributes and synthesizes; §8a adjudicates; the human coordinator rules or ratifies.
It never "convinces" a checker and never passes FORMATION_CORE to one.

## The worker-brief format

```
[TASK]        one paragraph; the deliverable's form; the docket line it discharges.
[DESIGN-INTENT] four lines — test-of / why-this-route / referent / failure-criterion —
              stating the DISPATCH intent. The AUTHOR owns the block in the probe
              script/memo, written at design time; THAT copy is the record, and a
              divergence between the two blocks is itself a finding (probes INDEX
              convention, 2026-08-13).
[HARD]        the exact anchors: primitive names (worker must read their docstrings —
              move (2)), R-NNN rows, paper §§, ledger entries. No bulk loads.
[IN-FORMATION] the curated extracts of CANDIDATE content this task touches, each with its
              governing record + current tier status + what is superseded.
[FENCES]      duties and rulings in force for this territory (e.g. boost-covariance duty;
              numerology guard; witness fences; scale/frame jurisdictions).
[CROSS]       digest of other workers' relevant advances since the worker's formation.
[KILL-TEST]   REQUIRED on every RESEARCH brief (2026-08-20, adopted from the zeta campaign's
              method — see the note in `knowledge/audit/consolidation_2026-08-18/`). **Name an
              object where the answer is KNOWN AND NEGATIVE, and state what the method must
              return on it.** If the method "succeeds" there, **the method is broken and the
              result is void however good it looks on the real target.** This is the
              false-positive control applied to RESEARCH, not only to checkers — the gap D-12
              names, generalized past its original scope.
              **TWT's available control worlds, non-exhaustive:** the **Z4 cubic lattice**, where
              the fourth-moment isotropy that D4 satisfies FAILS (residual 2, not 0) — any
              lattice argument that also works on Z4 has proved nothing about D4; the **MSSM
              spectrum**, the known-wrong particle content for our weak-mixing run-down; the
              **refuted readings in the negatives ledger** (the energetic speed-limit reading;
              the carrier-relative mass reading at face value) — a mechanism that revives one of
              them is refuted, not promising; and **§D.3.2's Spin(3) local state**, adjudicated
              drift, which any state-space argument must NOT reproduce.
              *A brief with no kill-test is a brief that cannot fail.*
              **★ UPGRADE FROM THE PRIMARY SOURCE (read 2026-08-20): THEY BUILT A TOOL, NOT A
              BRIEF FIELD.** The campaign's rung 0 was *"a mechanical barrier checker that
              classifies any proposed idea by which RH-false model kills it"*, maintained beside
              *"a zoo of RH-false model worlds"* — and it was **run against all 106 surviving
              ideas from the previous campaign and killed every one, mechanically reproducing a
              triage a human had done by hand.** A named control world in a brief is the weak
              form of this; **the strong form is a maintained zoo plus a checker that any
              proposed mechanism is run through before it is worked on.** Docketed as
              apparatus work — TWT's zoo already has members (Z4, the MSSM spectrum, the refuted
              readings, the Spin(3) drift) and no tool.
[PROFILE]     REQUIRED on every dispatch (RUL-069 as resolved by RUL-075, 2026-08-21): NEUTRAL ·
              PROSECUTORIAL only — the STEELMAN profile is REMOVED (RUL-044's double-work
              rejection stands at role level; the steelman function is now the WORKER'S OWN §8a
              consensus duty: steelman your position before conceding, question the adversarial
              review — see PROFILES.md header and FORMATION_CORE §2). Original axis text follows
              as history: NEUTRAL · PROSECUTORIAL ·
              STEELMAN · RISK-APPETITE. **The second axis on top of diet** — same diet, different
              disposition, and the same claim read under two profiles is strictly more information
              than one reading from a role made vaguely more positive. Unmarked = NEUTRAL. Full
              definitions and the loop redesign: `knowledge/prompts/PROFILES.md`.
              **The asymmetry this fixes:** counted honestly, every disposition the programme had
              built was prosecutorial — reviewer, meta-observer, keeper, contra-reviewer, removal
              auditor, enforcer. **Six prosecutors and no defence.**
[DIET]        REQUIRED on every dispatch (R-C, 2026-08-20): one line naming what this instance
              is SATURATED with and what it is STARVED of. **The whole architecture rests on the
              meta-observer being starved of the derivation, and until now NOTHING CHECKED THAT ANY
              DISPATCH ACTUALLY STARVED IT** — a brief could leak the derivation and the verdict
              would look identical. The enforcer verifies this line against what the brief
              contained. *A separation asserted and never verified is a convention, not a control.*
[DOUBTS]      OPTIONAL, and if present it MUST carry this sentence verbatim: *this list
              is not a boundary — findings outside it count fully.* (R3, adopted
              2026-08-19. Measured: six doubts were filed on the who-dies panel; the one
              the author worried most about was refuted by both checkers, while the
              governing defect and a category error went unflagged. A doubt list steers
              checkers toward what the author already suspects.)
[FORECAST]    REQUIRED on every dispatch (2026-08-20, from the zeta campaign's practice): the
              coordinator's own expectation of what this dispatch returns, **stated before it
              runs.** Their coordinator launched a six-rung ladder *"with its expectations stated
              in advance: rungs 0-2 can return theorems/tools; 3 is a real shot; 4/5 is a
              moonshot and I'm labelling it that going in."* This is [PREDICTION] generalized
              from measurements to **all** dispatches, and it costs one line. **Its value is
              that a wrong forecast is legible afterwards** — and on that campaign's own
              account, the coordinator's forecast was WRONG at both creative steps, which is
              how the wins were recognised as wins.
[PREDICTION]  REQUIRED whenever the task is a measurement or an audit whose outcome the
              program has a stake in: the dated expectation, written BEFORE the run
              (R4, adopted 2026-08-19). Converts an expression into a test at near-zero
              cost. Report the outcome against it either way.
[BANKING]     what this task may bank (usually: nothing — report as CANDIDATE with
              pre-registered acceptance tests; §8a before any banking).
```

**★ A BRIEF IS A RESEARCH MEMO, NOT A TASK TICKET — AND ITS STEER IS A HYPOTHESIS THE WORKER MAY
INVERT (2026-08-20, from the zeta campaign's primary account).** Their briefs carried *"the target,
a template, the coordinator's conjectures and its forecast of the outcome"* — and **both** creative
steps of the campaign came from an agent **contradicting its brief's steer**: one was told to bound
an index from above, found that route *empty*, and inverted it; the other was handed a diagnosis
and a wrong forecast of the recovery, and produced the winning inequality **against the steer**.
The coordinator's own summary is that at each step it *"pointed the mechanism the wrong way."*

**Two consequences for how this role writes briefs.** (1) **Give the worker enough structure to
invert** — a thin ticket cannot be argued with, so it cannot be productively contradicted; the
conjecture and the forecast are what make inversion possible. (2) **A worker reporting that the
briefed route is empty, and returning a different one, is a SUCCESS mode and is to be received as
one** — not as non-compliance. Say so in the brief.

**★ THE CROSS-DOMAIN LEVER (human coordinator, 2026-08-27 — RUL-111; C-34 in RULES_CORE).** Every
RESEARCH brief carries one line reminding the worker that its comparative strength over the human
literature is CROSS-DOMAIN REACH — training spanning essentially all of physics and mathematics
where human specialists command one branch — and explicitly licenses ranging: where the
coordinator can, the brief names one or two candidate out-of-branch levers as [HARD] anchors
(an analogue system, an adjacent formalism's theorem), and a worker's out-of-branch route is
received as a success mode exactly like a steer inversion. Fences unchanged: cross-domain
imports register like any import, prior art is read not paraphrased (F3), and an analogue is a
lever, not a derivation.

## Dispatch tiers — full ceremony vs the light path (R-G, 2026-08-20)

**The apparatus had ONE INTENSITY**: a banking-bound claim and a throwaway probe ran the same
ceremony, and there was no cheap mode. Two tiers now:

- **FULL CEREMONY — anything banking-bound.** The complete brief format, §8a routing, persisted
  verdicts, registry rows. Unchanged.
- **LIGHT PATH — exploration that CANNOT bank.** A short brief ([TASK] + [DIET] + [FENCES] + the
  banking line), no §8a round, no registry rows, and its output enters as **CANDIDATE** by
  construction. **This is safe for exactly one reason, and the reason is the whole justification:
  the guarantees only need to hold AT THE BANK GATE**, and everything upstream of it is non-binding
  by construction. A light-path result that later wants to bank re-enters at full ceremony —
  **it does not get grandfathered.**

**Mark the tier in the brief's first line.** An unmarked dispatch is full ceremony.

**And the half that is the coordinator's own to fix:** much of the measured cost is the **dispatch
pattern, not the rules** — a probe-scale dispatch pays roughly **20,000 tokens of fixed
canon-plus-prefix overhead to do ~600 tokens of work**. Load the prefix conditionally: the light
path takes `RULES_CORE.md` plus its role pack, not the full formation prefix.

## Cache architecture (why this design is cheap)

FORMATION_CORE is the byte-stable shared prefix (re-written only at consolidation; cached —
subsequent reads ~10% price). Role instructions and briefs are small suffixes. The
hard/in-formation content boundary IS the cache boundary: hard = prefix, in-formation =
per-brief suffix. Consolidation (arc-close: banking triage + archivist pass) = the one
moment both the corpus and the prefix version move together.

   brief, not a tombstone** — and nobody will notice that it is met unless someone deliberately
   looks, because the ledger's own framing points the other way.
